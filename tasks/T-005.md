## Report (Freebuff, 2026-11-10)

Branch `T-005`. Deliverable: `docs/t005-frame-pacing.md`, plus
`scripts/t005_pacing.py` and `re/t005-sleep-sites.csv`.

### Findings

1. **No 33 ms sleep exists.** The 21 direct `sys_timer_usleep` (141) sites all
   pass 10–1200 µs. All game sleeping funnels through a helper at `0x8a9458`
   (`sleep(seconds)` → `usleep(round(sec*1e6))`, 30 µs floor, rate word
   `0x8a9438` = 1e6f). Its 70 call sites pass either a heap-object interval or
   `0.0` / `0.01` / `1.0` s — never 1/30. The timer-syscall framing of the cap
   is therefore refuted.
2. **The six code-side `1/30` words are GCC literal pools**, and only two have
   readers: `0x76ae54` (Bezier/curve math: `fnmsubs`/`fsel`) and `0xfed528`
   (startup wait loop in `sub_87917c`, sleeps 1/30 until global `0x1aafc2c` is
   set). The other four are unreferenced. The "six 1/30 D-line bake sites"
   lead from T-004 §6 is closed — patching them cannot raise fps.
3. **`0x160bbf0` explained.** The double 2^32 that T-004 rejected as a decoy is
   the timebase scaler in the timestamp helper `sub_879034` (`mftb` → double
   seconds, stored at `0x1a75088`). It must never be patched.
4. **Probes P1/P2** in the doc: no-op the sleep helper (`be32 0x8a9458
   0x4e800020`) and halve all sleeps (`bef32 0x8a9438 500000.0`), each with
   pre-declared outcomes; plus a 4-step debugger recipe (sleep helper,
   timestamp helper, import stub `0x16037c0` / slot `0x195a3d0`, flip path).

### Honest status
No proven 60 fps patch. This task removed two hypotheses with evidence and
narrowed the mechanism to a blocking wait, with one boot needed to tell a
sleep-shaped cap from an event-shaped one. Agnes wires + boot-verifies; a
"fps unchanged" P1 result should come back to me rather than being patched
around.
